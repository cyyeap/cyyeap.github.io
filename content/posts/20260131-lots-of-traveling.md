It's been a while since I wrote the blog posts.

I stopped writing them because it was hard when I was travelling. Honestly, it was just an excuse. This post is going to be a long one, as I'm a bit too lazy to break it down, so I'm just going to consolidate all the travel posts together.

Since quitting my job, I had travelled across the globe, from Bali, KL, London, Chicago, Vegas, and back home in Melbourne for a two-week break before going on a long vacation. A short break between vacations.
I had been travelling a lot since I got my first job. My travels were not that expensive because I don't usually care what kind of accommodation/hotel I stay in, but since being with Aidan, he's changed me to enjoy a slightly more luxurious travelling style, and I realised I've changed a bit on that front. As I think about what holds true to me in every aspect of life, it's this: moderation is the key to everything. Beliefs, expenditure, experiences, opinions.

One thing I don't really want to change about travelling is spontaneity. When I first started travelling, I would book everything in advance, like six months ahead. These days, I barely plan, and I just plan on the go. I like it this way for several reasons (exceptions apply, of course; if I were travelling during peak season, I'd still plan a little):
* Usually accommodation is cheaper on that day.
* Sometimes the city is much more boring than I expected, and my flexibility allows me to leave the city anytime I want to.
* Meeting new people during the trip: if I've already booked something else, changing plans is difficult. I've made so many good friends from my travels, because the flexibility allows me to travel with them.

This always freaks a lot of my friends out. Funnily, once they've done one trip with me, they start to see that it's actually okay.
Bali

I wrote many posts about Bali, so I'm not going to post more about it.

KL
* It's been a while (like maybe 5+ years) since I visited KL.
* It was primarily to visit my high school friend.
* I stayed in a Harry Potter-themed Airbnb; it was decorated in a very creative way.
* As I grow older, I'm a lot more sensitive to noise/light, and it disrupts my sleep easily. The Airbnb was a mistake for me; I'd avoid these days and choose hotels for better sound insulation.
* I learnt that the Hilton KL was situated in such a good location, next to KL Central, and thought that next time I come to KL, I'd stay at the Hilton instead.

Qatar
* First time on Qatar business class! Loved the entire experience, and loved the Starlink connection, super fast, but I quite liked that I could disconnect myself from the world while on non-wifi flights.
* The business class lounge in Doha was amazing; it's sooo luxuriously spacious.
* My favourite business class experience. The food was good, the service was good, and the amenities kit was good. (Singapore Airlines was surprisingly pretty lacklustre, tried a few times with newer planes as well.)

London was amazing; it's my favourite city by far, albeit I didn't appreciate the winter that much.

* Spent a couple of days combating jetlag.
* I didn't know how expensive London was. I've heard of it being one of the most expensive cities to live in, and Melbourne is up there as well, but it's a lot more expensive than I thought.
The short-term accommodation was expensive, and I suspect Aidan and I might end up buying a small apartment within Zone 1/2 of London given how much I like it, and wish that my friends and family aren't deterred by the fact that it's expensive to visit, and rent it out on Airbnb when it isn't in use.
* Before embarking on the travelling journey, I caught up with Heaven and found out she'd be in London while I was there, so it was extremely nice to travel with a friend.
* I didn't go to any non-museum landmarks in London :D
* I spent most of my time scrolling through museums and galleries, and loved the British Museum a lot!
* I made some new friends while I was there, while shopping! I met a lovely young lady called August while I was shopping for some indie designer clothes, and thought we could be friends and asked her to hang out with me and Heaven.

British Airways
* So, I flew British Airways for the first time in first class. It was AUD 23K if I had purchased the ticket.
(Okay, Aidan and I don't usually pay for premium+ class when we're travelling. It's just outright too splurgy to use cash to get business class tickets.
Aidan and I have accumulated A LOT of Amex points throughout paying our taxes, and first class on British Airways required fewer points than business class, so I went for it.
Aidan flew first class last year to the US from the UK, and I flew economy to the US to meet him, and he thought it was only fair for me to fly first class this time, and he's still flying business class this time to meet me. Lol.)

Chicago
* It was very cold.
* It was a neat and relatively clean city, relative to other American cities.
* The architecture was amazing.

Vegas / Arizona
* Really enjoyed being in the canyons.
* Hated the vibe of Las Vegas.
* We got to stay at the Venetian for free, by far, the biggest hotel room I've stayed in a western country (the biggest was in China!). 
* We'd had to pay USD 30 to use the kettle at the Venetian's hotel room, so bring your own kettle to the USA if you're staying at an American hotel!
* The best place I enjoyed in Vegas was the Writer's Block; I spent three hours there.
* Vegas was super dry; I had to buy multiple bottles of moisturiser. The lip balms from British Airways were finally useful.

Melbourne, two-week break
* I love living in Melbourne so much.
* I bought a place two years ago, and that has made me love living here more now, seeing the improvements I've made to my home.
* I started doing yoga almost every day, and I really enjoy yoga now.
* I still hadn't done any bookings for the next trip except the ticket to Singapore and the ticket back from Shanghai. Nothing planned in between.
* Since I'd recently had a big trip, I decided the next one should be more relaxing.
* I had to visit Penang once last year; that's the only requirement for the next trip, and my sister's family will be in China, and Aidan and I decided to tag along.

KL
* I was tossing up between Bangkok and KL, but didn't get to see much of KL in November, so I decided to come back here again.
* Yes, I decided we should stay at the Hilton KL. It's more expensive than Airbnbs, but it's well worth every cent.
* I didn't know Aidan had Hilton Gold membership via Amex, so I booked via Booking.com and we missed the upgrade, but they still gave us complimentary breakfast for two.
* It was the best breakfast we'd had at a hotel; the food was amazing, and there were so many options. Imagine, they had lok see fan... so yummy!
* Bought a lot of art from A4 Art Gallery.

Penang
* We landed in Penang during Christmas. It was a mistake not to book the flight/bus/train tickets in advance. Train and bus were fully booked, so we resorted to taking a flight via Subang; it's the first propeller flight Aidan has taken. It was AUD 500 for two tickets, which is way too expensive, but alas, I needed to see my parents.
* The hotel in Penang was also more expensive than on my last trips that I could recall. Should always book ahead.
